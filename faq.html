Help Center

Your questions, answered.

Find quick answers to the most common questions about our reviews and recommendations.

What is PickYourProduct?

+

PickYourProduct is a product discovery and review platform that helps you make smarter buying decisions. We research and curate only the top-performing products across categories like Electronics, Fitness & Health, Home Appliances, and Beauty & Skincare.

Instead of overwhelming you with hundreds of options, we highlight only the products that are actually worth your money.

How do you choose the products you recommend?

+

We use a combination of factors to select the best products:

  • Real-world testing and hands-on experience
  • Analysis of thousands of customer reviews
  • Price-to-performance ratio and value for money
  • Brand reputation and product reliability
  • Expert opinions and industry standards

We update our recommendations regularly to ensure you get the most current and accurate information.

Are your reviews unbiased and independent?

+

Yes, absolutely. Our reviews are completely independent and unbiased. We do not accept payment or free products in exchange for positive reviews.

Our recommendations are based solely on product quality, performance, and value. We only recommend products we genuinely believe are worth your money.

What are affiliate links? Do they affect your recommendations?

+

Some links on our website are affiliate links. This means if you click on a link and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you.

Important: Our affiliate relationships do not influence our recommendations. We only recommend products we genuinely believe in, regardless of whether we earn a commission. These commissions help us keep the website running and provide free, high-quality content for our readers.

How often do you update your recommendations?

+

We update our product recommendations regularly — typically every 1-3 months. We also update listings whenever:

  • A better product launches in the same price range
  • Prices change significantly
  • New customer feedback reveals important issues
  • A product becomes unavailable or discontinued

You can check the "Updated" date on each article to see when it was last reviewed.

Can I suggest a product or category for review?

+

Absolutely! We love hearing from our readers. If there's a product or category you'd like us to review, please reach out through our Contact page.

Your suggestions help us improve and cover the products you actually care about.

How can I stay updated with new reviews?

+

The easiest way to stay updated is to visit our homepage regularly. We also recommend:

  • Bookmarking our website for quick access
  • Following our social media channels (coming soon)
  • Checking back weekly as we add new reviews every week

Do you ship products or sell directly?

+

No, PickYourProduct is a review and recommendation platform only. We do not sell or ship products directly. When you click on a "Buy" link, you'll be redirected to trusted e-commerce platforms like Amazon, Flipkart, or the brand's official website where you can complete your purchase securely.

We're here to help. Get in touch and we'll get back to you within 24–48 hours.
